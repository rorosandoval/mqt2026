4th Workshop on Molecular Quantum Technology - MQT 2026

◈ 14-18th December 2026, Puerto Varas, Chile

MQT 2026 is a unique workshop that brings together researchers across disciplines to discuss fundamental science and applications of molecules and molecular materials for next-generation quantum technology.

Previous MQT workshops have attracted an engaging group of renowned scientists and early career researchers from physics, chemistry and materials science, with common interests in this growing area of quantum science. Contributions from students and postdocs are strongly encouraged.

The Venue

MQT 2026 will be held at the Dreams hotel in the beautiful lake city of Puerto Varas, the northern entry point of the Chilean patagonia.
